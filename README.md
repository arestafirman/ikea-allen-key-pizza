## Entry

> Try our new topping: one IKEA Allen key, baked into the crust — it comes with a 47-page manual in Swedish and no refund.

120 characters, one line, no trailing newline.

## Why this angle

The field is saturated with wet socks, toothpaste, mayonnaise, and anchovies — the usual "gross food" answers. Nobody is doing furniture or flat-pack assembly. An Allen key baked into the crust is a hardware topping with a real consequence: you need the manual to eat it, the manual is in Swedish, and there is no refund. The joke lands in two beats — the absurd topping, then the bureaucratic punchline — and it stays inside the pizza world instead of reaching for another gross-out ingredient. The worst topping is not something that tastes bad; it is something you have to assemble.
